# StreamStudio D2C: LIV Golf Edition — Executive Presentation Outline

Boardroom-ready narrative for Chief Media Officers and VPs of Digital Products.

---

## Slide 1: The Problem — The Third-Party Streaming Split

**Headline:** The Cost of Passive Distribution Networks

**Key points:**

- Traditional broadcast syndicators and third-party media outlets (Twitch, YouTube, legacy TV) absorb between 30% and 50% of direct user monetization revenue.
- Audience viewing habits and consumer data tracking records stay locked inside third-party databases, blinding the sports property.
- Shotgun start models suffer from coverage blind spots; traditional single-feed arrangements cannot track multiple active group pairings simultaneously.

---

## Slide 2: The Solution — StreamStudio Direct-to-Consumer (D2C)

**Headline:** Total Broadcast Command & 100% Revenue Retention

**Key points:**

- A fully white-labeled native application framework delivering high-performance, low-latency live streams natively on owned infrastructure.
- LIV Golf retains 100% of fan token transactions, e-commerce purchases, and digital sponsor contracts.
- Integrated cloud-based mixing panels and overlay modules let production teams deploy real-time fan engagement loops directly over the active video container.

---

## Slide 3: Core Feature Architecture — In-Stream Micro-Bets

**Headline:** Capitalizing on Broadcast Downtime

**Key points:**

- **In-Stream Micro-Bets:** Keeps viewers locked into the player interface between shots by pushing fast, point-by-point prop bets (e.g., “Will Bryson clear this bunker?”).
- **Frictionless Sliding Drawers:** Viewers submit token tickets natively inside the live video frame canvas without dropping or interrupting the stream feed.
- **Sportsbook Direct Path:** The underlying module can be white-labeled straight to an official wagering partner (e.g., DraftKings, BetMGM), turning the player into a highly valuable sponsorship asset.

---

## Slide 4: Operations Framework — Edge-Level Geo-Compliance

**Headline:** Security and Jurisdictional Safety Built Into the Core

**Key points:**

- **CDN Boundary Interception:** Evaluates viewer geolocation metrics at the edge level using spatial coordinates before processing balance modifications or layout views.
- **Polygon Matching:** Dynamic point-in-polygon checks ensure that wagering components deploy exclusively within valid geographic borders, automatically falling back to clean entertainment modes elsewhere.
- **Fail-Closed Architecture:** If location indicators are modified, tracking layers drop, or proxies are detected, transactional pipelines shut down instantly to guarantee compliance.

---

## Slide 5: Financial Realities & Operational Roadmap

**Headline:** Value Engineering and Scalability Timeline

**Key points:**

- **Phase 1 Deployment (Operational Today):** Fully functional, integrated cloud studio controllers, database synchronization layers, and real token transaction engines running natively inside infrastructure.
- **Phase 2 Automation Upgrade:** Integrating automated odds data hooks (Sportradar / Genius Sports webhooks) to dynamically price and settle micro-bets with no manual production labor overhead.
- **Commercial Model Options:** White-label licensing agreements ($150k–$500k configuration setup + flat monthly host fee) or clear revenue partnership alternatives based on digital transaction scale.
